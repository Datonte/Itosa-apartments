Quick Answers

Common questions, answered

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in starts at 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Need flexibility? Reach out via WhatsApp before booking.
Do you have backup power?
Yes — every apartment has inverter backup that runs lights, fans, internet, and a single AC during outages.
How do I pay?
Securely online via Paystack. We accept Nigerian debit/credit cards, bank transfer, and USSD. No hidden fees.
Can I cancel?
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. After that, the first night is non-refundable. See our refund policy.
